Weekly Reports

Week 1

During Week 1, we discussed the Raider Trainer project and what the goals of the project are. We disscussed are strengths and weaknesses when it comes to coding and decided our roles in the project. We decided that Aaron will work on creating the Team Website and creating the Raider Trainer desktop application. Zander will work on getting the pose trainer working. Alan will work on the back-end coding for the application.

Week 2

We started and finished the requirements excel sheet. We talked through and assigned what each of us are going to do for the project. Aaron created the team website and made rough edits to start. Zander and Alan worked on getting the pose trainer working on their systems.

Week 3

Aaron got the users and admin account working on the web application. Each user can log in and select the workout that they want to do. The admin account can unlock locked out users and change their passwords. Alan got the pose tracker to actually track the angles of the arm and to track x and y coordinates. Zander implemented functions to track the reps and sets done by the user. Aaron will work on getting the database create to track the workout history of the users. Alan will work on getting the pose tracker to determine correct and incorrect reps. Zander will work on the weight tracking function that will identify the weight that the user wants to use.
